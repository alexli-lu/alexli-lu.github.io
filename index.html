I’m a PhD student in Business Economics and Public Policy at the Ivey Business School, Western University. I work in applied microeconomics, using panel data and text-as-data methods to study how public policy shapes the behaviour of governments and firms. I completed my MA in Economics and my BCom in Finance & Economics at the University of Toronto.
